## 1. How the night was measured

Every number below comes from a clean copy of one commit (`git archive <commit>`), never from the work tree. One
measure set is these commands, with the seeds of set A or of set B:

```sh
# Gate G1: fatalbot against the reference bots. Set A: seeds 1,2. Set B: seeds 3,4.
node tools/arena.js --a fatal --b ref --maps de_dust2,de_inferno,de_train,de_aztec --rounds 12 --seeds 1,2 --sides both
# Gates G2 to G4: fatalbot against itself. Set A: seeds 1 to 6. Set B: seeds 7 to 12.
node tools/arena.js --a fatal --b fatal --maps de_dust2,de_inferno,de_nuke,de_train,de_aztec,de_cbble \
  --rounds 12 --seeds 1,2,3,4,5,6 --sides once
# The recorded matches, for de_dust2, de_inferno, de_nuke and de_train. Set A: seed 1. Set B: seed 2 (other demos).
node tools/demo-arena.js --map <map> --count 6 --rounds 10 --seed 1
node tools/demo-arena.js --map <map> --count 6 --rounds 10 --seed 1 --standins fatal
```

Set A at `47adee4` gives the numbers of the earlier documents again, to the round: one command gives one result.

**One fault of the game core was in every run of de_nuke until the last hour of the night.** csbih-player moves
a door by the origin of its hull model. Every world of a level shares that model, and a new world takes the
place where the last world left a door as the door's closed place. So on de_nuke (17 doors) a match depended on
the matches before it in the same process: seed 8 of fatalbot against itself ended 2:10 after seeds 1 to 7 and
3:9 alone. This is why one build with one set of seeds gives the Ts 54.0% in section 0 (two processes of six
seeds for each map) and 53.2% in section 6 (one process of twelve): the two differ on de_nuke only. The other
five maps agree to the round (de_inferno has one door, the others none). The host now puts the doors back before each
new world (`c6655af`, adapter.js `restLevel`). Section 0 has the gates with it; the numbers of de_nuke in the
sections after it are from before it. The fix belongs in csbih-player too (`doors.js doorsOf`): the page makes
many worlds of one level.

**How much a number says.** The rounds of a match are not 12 samples. The money of a lost round makes the next
round harder, so a match goes 11:1 or 3:9. Blocks of 72 rounds of one build on one map (six matches, other seeds)
gave 41, 50, 49, 51, 59 and 47 rounds to the Ts: a spread of about 6 rounds. A change that draws one more random
number plays other rounds from then on, so a block of 72 rounds can differ by 15 rounds with no cause. A
difference on one map counts here only when it repeats on seeds that the change was not chosen on.

## 2. The bots that take over for a player: stuck (gate G3)

The takeover test of docs/measures/2026-11-10-replay-takeover.md showed bots that were stuck 6.2% of the time on
de_train and 2.5% on de_nuke. The guess of that document (a player stands where the graph has no floor) was
wrong. A log of every stuck spell, with its place, its goal and the seconds since the takeover, gave three other
causes:

| Cause | Where | Stuck seconds of 120 rounds |
|-------|-------|------|
| At the top of a ladder beside its floor the bot turned its view to the floor. The game moves a player on a ladder along the view, so the turn sent it down again. It swung between two heights. | de_train, one ladder | 607 of 703 |
| The floors of a platform have no way in the graph but one, at a ladder's top. The bot walked to that floor, then searched its way from the floor nearest to it, which has no way. It shook in one place. | de_nuke, the platform over the upper site | 243 of 377 |
| A climb holds no stuck clock. A ladder that the bot cannot take (under a hatch that only a ducked player fits) was never marked as failed: after each give-up the bot found the same way. | de_nuke, the hatch of the roof | 76 of 377 |

The changes, one commit each:

- `49c6db7`: at a ladder's top with the floor on the open side of the ladder's face, the bot keeps its face to
  the ladder, climbs until its feet clear the floor, then steps off sideways. A floor straight behind it takes a
  jump off the ladder.
- `97c8874`: a bot on an island of the graph starts its way from the joined floor that it stands at. Of the joined
  floors it takes the first that a straight walk reaches (the nearest can lie behind a wall). A bot on an island
  counts as able to reach the level, so it gets a goal there.
- `59c88e9`: a give-up marks the step of the way that the bot stood before, as a stuck walk does.

| Stuck seconds on de_train, 120 rounds | 703 | 59 |

## 7. A fault of the data: a recording named after the next map

One recording of the second seed set gave a bot that was stuck 97% of the time. Its players stood inside walls.
The recording is a match on de_dust2. The proxy stayed on the server when it changed to de_nuke and recorded 100
more seconds there, and the import of csbih-player names a timeline after the last map of its demo.

**A correction.** The first rule for this (`9aed11e`) left out every round in which half of the players start
inside a wall: 105 rounds. This document and the report of that morning said that those recordings go on into
another map. A look at each of them shows that this was wrong for most:

| What it is | Recordings | Rounds |
|------|------|------|
| A match on de_dust2 under the name de_nuke: the server changed map at its end | 1 | 28 |
| A recording of protocol 46 on the 1.6 build of de_aztec: the tools took the 1.5 build by the protocol | 1 | 21 |
| de_prodigy in a build that csbih-player does not have | 2 | 19 |
| Rounds of the right map whose players stand at their spawns, a few units inside the floor at the first sample | 43 | about 50 |
| Single rounds whose players start in a wall, far from every spawn (two of them are recordings of one round) | 7 | 7 |

The rules now:

- `strategies.js fitRounds` (`ee12017`): a round is of another map when half of its players or more start inside
  a wall and further than 96 units from every spawn of the level. A recording with most of its rounds far from
  every spawn keeps only the rounds at the spawns. Each sign alone is wrong: players at their spawns can start a
  few units inside the floor, and the start of a round can be read late.
- `load.js levelFor` (`d170cec`): the build of a recording's level is the build of its protocol, or the other
  build when most rounds start at that build's spawns. The 21 rounds of de_aztec are back.
- Of 14,744 rounds the tools leave out 54. docs/strategies.md and the players' aim are made again: 98,538 kills
  with a gun, 306 fewer than in docs/measures/2026-11-10-players-aim.md, and no number of that document moves by
  more than its error (the kills with a shot to the head stay at 42.1%, the first sight to the first shot at
  0.38 s, the crosshair error at the first shot at 2.0 degrees).

**The cut of the one recording belongs in csbih-player**, which this repository does not change. It is ready as
a patch, `out/csbih-player/cut-map-change.patch` (not committed, not applied): `src/map-change.js` finds the
map of a recording by the spawns at which its rounds start and cuts off what follows the change of map,
`tools/cut-map-change.js` does it for the timelines in `out/`, and a test. Checked in a copy of csbih-player made
