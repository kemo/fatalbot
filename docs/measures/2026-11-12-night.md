- **The base of the night** is commit `362beb4` (the commits after it to `2f16a96` change documents only). Every

## 1. What the players do after the first death (a measure, no change)

Three agents measured the recorded matches beside the bots; they changed no code. For the time from the first
death of a round to the plant, on de_dust2, de_inferno, de_nuke and de_train (10,814 recorded rounds; 288 rounds
of the bots, seeds 7 to 12):

| Measure | Recorded CTs | Bot CTs | Recorded Ts | Bot Ts |
|---------|------|------|------|------|
| A step of 2 s in which the player stands | 17% | 36% | 10% | 20% |
| Windows of 10 s with 200 units or more between start and end | 72% | 23% | 84% | 68% |
| Windows of 10 s with over 600 units between start and end | 40% | 10% | 52% | 41% |
| Stands, in the windows under 200 units | 44% | 42% | 34% | 40% |
| Stands, in the windows of 200 units or more | 8% | 19% | 5% | 13% |

- **The recorded players travel after the first death.** A player that stays about one place stands as often as a
  bot does (44% against 42% for the CTs). The gap is that the bot CTs stay: they hold a post in 89% of that time.
- **Where the travel goes** (windows over 600 units): toward the site where the attack is in 52% of the CTs'
  windows, toward the place of a teammate's death in 51% of the windows that have one.
- **The bot Ts** are still at the waiting place of their plan in 35% of the steps after the first death.
- **Close to an enemy** (under 500 units) a recorded CT stands in 19% of the steps and a bot CT in 57%. Nothing
  was changed for that: steps at every moment of a wait cost 19 rounds on 2026-11-11.

## 2. A part of a rotation (`5c523db`, kept)

The CTs rotated only when the team saw 60% of the living Ts at one site within 6 s, all at once, and never back.
Now the first T that the team sees at a site (or the killer of a teammate there) brings the defenders of the other
site over: the nearest first, one a second, and one of them stays. They go back when no T was seen at that site
for 20 s, or when the team sees an attack at another site. The whole team still rotates at 60%.

The rule, written before the run: on set B a CT stands in 30% of the steps after the first death or less (the
base 36%), and the Ts' share by match stays inside gate G2; over both sets gate G1 does not fall by more than 5
points, the places of recorded CTs and Ts not by more than 10 rounds each, the takeover side not by more than 20
rounds, and stuck and wall rise by no more than 0.1 point.

## 4. The bomb play of the players, and the CTs' save (`8b64263`, kept)

An agent measured the play after a plant in the recorded matches (4,762 plants on de_dust2, de_inferno, de_train
and de_nuke) beside the bots (913 plants, 31 seeds):

| Measure | The recorded players | The bots before |
|---------|------|------|
| Plants after which a CT is alive at the blast | 16% | 2% |
| The same with the Ts two or more ahead at the plant, one ahead, level | 23%, 18%, 11% | 2%, 2%, 2% |
| The CTs win after a plant with the Ts two or more ahead | 2% | 1% |

| 10 s after the plant: the living Ts within 500 units of the bomb | 34% | 73% |

- **The players decide a retake by the count of players.** The bots stopped only on the clock.
- **A recorded CT that saves** last moves toward the bomb about 19 s after the plant, about 2,100 units from it,
  and is about 2,600 units from it at the blast. 39% stay where they are and 35% move away.
- **The recorded Ts stand off their bomb**, and leave it in the last 7 s. Nothing was changed for that yet.

Two rows of `tools/rounds.js` are new (`5d4226a`, `4f79b51`): the plants after which the Ts win and a CT is alive
at the end, and the Ts near the bomb 10 s after the plant. The first form of the first row read the last sample
of a round, up to 2 s before its end, and gave 69%; the row now counts the players at the round's end event.

Now the CT team calls a save after a plant by the count of players alive, drawn once for each count: a chance of
8% with the Ts one ahead and 22% with two or more. A CT within 500 units of the bomb plays on; a teammate at the
bomb ends the call. A CT that saves leaves the blast (1,500 units) and holds a place with its view to the bomb.

## 5. A bot on a route: the trap on de_nuke and the stand point out of reach (`f239d20`, `2ac8fb3`, kept)

An agent split the stuck and wall time of the bots that play on for a recorded player (`--standins fatal-route`,
948 rounds, 35,715 s on a route) by what the bot did:

| State of the bot | Stuck above the bots with no route | At a wall above them |
|---------|------|------|
| On its route, where the player stood | 117 s | 324 s |
| On its route, where the player moved | 13 s | 100 s |
| On a walk back to the route by the graph | 198 s | 89 s |
| After it left the route | 463 s | 541 s |

- **The wall time on a route is the players'.** The recorded track itself faces a wall as often at the same
  moments (6.05% against the bot's 6.17% where the player stood): about 424 s of the 1,056 s.
- **The largest source is a trap on de_nuke.** A route takes a bot onto a floor that the graph of csbih-player
  has no way out of (seven such places, all on de_nuke; one has 100 floors, one 29, five have one). After the
  bot left its route there, the walk chose the nearest floor of the wanted part, behind a wall, and the bot
  walked at that wall for the rest of the round: 663 s, 36% of the extra time. Each such bot stood against
  world walls under a low ceiling: a jump, a duck or a lower floor frees none of them.
- **Where the player stood**, the bot kept its keys pressed toward a point that its box does not reach.

Two changes:

1. `walk.js`: a bot on an island first asks the 32 nearest floors of the wanted part, as before; then every such
   floor within 640 units (found once for a floor of the island); then the island's own floors, nearest first by
   the island's ways, for one with a straight walk out, and goes there by the island's ways.
2. `route.js`: a routed bot that gets no nearer to the place where its player stood for 0.4 s, stands.

The rule, written before the run (both sets judge; no number was chosen on a set): with routes the stand-ins'
stuck and wall shares fall by a fifth or more; the time on a route does not fall by more than 3%; the side with
the routes does not win fewer rounds by more than 20; the other limits as in section 2.

| With routes: the stand-ins are stuck | 1.47% | 1.51% | 1.11% |
| With routes: the stand-ins face a wall | 1.52% | 1.52% | 1.21% |
| The same on de_nuke, stuck and wall | 2.78%, 3.39% | 2.94%, 3.14% | 2.01%, 2.07% |
| Seconds on a route | 35,715 | 34,489 | 34,460 |

- Stuck falls by 27% and wall by 21% against the build before.
- **One point of the rule fails as it was written:** against the base of the night the time on a route is 3.5%
  shorter (the rule allowed 3%). The build before these changes has that loss already (34,489 s): it comes from
  the team rules of the night, with which a full team meets the routed bots sooner. The two changes themselves
