## 2. The measure tools (F8, F6 report)

- The head share of the bots divided by all kills. The players' share (`players.js playerFacts`) leaves out the
  knife and the grenades. Both now use `gunKill`, and so do the aim engagements of the bots.
- "G4 plants" divided the rounds with a plant by the rounds of both kinds. `measureLine` now prints "G4 plants as
  the Ts" for each kind. The line "G4 plants" that `sum.mjs` of csbih-player prints from `s.plants` keeps the
  old count of both kinds: read the line of each kind.
- G5 counts each command into bins of 4.4% that add up over the processes of a run, and prints the p99 and the
  count over 2 ms beside the mean. A base run has no bins: its p99 prints as "-".

- The budget changes the play, so the two matches of a map are not the same match: compare the shares. On de_nuke
  the commands over 2 ms go from 52 to 32 in 100,000, the p99 from 0.16 to 0.09 ms, and the mean from 0.016 to
  0.013 ms. On de_dust2 the counts are too small to tell.
- The longest command did not go away. On de_dust2 one command of the later match takes 6.5 to 6.7 ms in each
  run: a single search. A single search of 20,000 to 60,000 floors (a way, the time of a T's way to its site)
  costs several milliseconds alone, and the budget does not split one search.
- A tick of 10 ms holds the commands of ten bots. With the budget no command pays for two or three searches
  (section 4), so a tick over 10 ms needs two such searches by two bots in it.
