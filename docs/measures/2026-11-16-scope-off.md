# 2026-11-16: a sniper takes the scope off after the fight

The owner played a demo match of de_train in a room of csbih-player and reported: "some walked around with scope /
double scope".

## 1. The cause

A bot looks through the scope only in a fight (`fight.js`: an enemy over 400 units away). Nothing took the scope
off after the fight:

- `fight()` returns before the scope code when the enemy was not seen for 0.15 s. The brain keeps the fight for
  2.5 s (`MEMORY`), and after that it does not call the fight. The walk then had the feet with the scope on.
- A near fight (under 400 units) presses the right button on a scope at the first level. The game goes to the
  second level, and the fight never pressed again once the enemy was out of sight.
- After a shot the game brings the scope back when the bolt is worked. After a kill this happened with no fight.

The new test `test/fight.test.js` (an AWP bot whose enemy goes behind a wall) shows it. Before the change, the
scope was still on 15 s after the loss.

## 2. The change

`eb6e2e4`: a bot with a sniper rifle presses the right button when the feet are not the fight's, 1 s after the last
sight, and after the bolt. From the first level the press goes through the second level for 0.3 s, as the game
cycles the scope.

## 3. The measures

**In a room of csbih-player.** A replay of one demo match of de_train (25 rounds; csbih-player branch
`bots-scope-off`, the member stands still; the script counts each tick of each bot that left its recording with a
sniper rifle in the hand). The replay script is in no repository: it opens a `Room` of csbih-player from
`folderSource`, enters one member that sends no command, runs `room.tick()` to the end of the match and counts:

| | before (`6815595`) | after (`eb6e2e4`) |
|---|---|---|
| ticks on the move (over 30 units a second) with the scope on, hit boxes of the model | 1486 of 26780 | 296 of 22555 |
| the same, the room's own boxes | 4664 of 28407 | 220 of 24424 |
| ticks at the second level, hit boxes of the model | 2 | 122 |

The second level now shows on the way off: 0.3 s for each press, about four in a match.

**The demo arena.** `tools/demo-arena.js` gives the bots the guns of their players, AWPs too. The arena of
`tools/arena.js` cannot see this change: its bots never buy a sniper rifle (`buy.js`).

```sh
node tools/demo-arena.js --map de_train --count 4 --rounds 30 --seed <1|2> --standins fatal-route
```

Each run from `git archive` of the commit, with `CSBIH_PLAYER` at the csbih-player checkout (main `3ab9fa8`).

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| fatalbot wins in the place of the Ts, seed 1, seed 2 | 65 of 102, 54 of 97 | 62 of 102, 53 of 97 |
| fatalbot wins in the place of the CTs, seed 1, seed 2 | 48 of 102, 58 of 97 | 48 of 102, 56 of 97 |
| both seeds | 225 of 398 (56.5%) | 219 of 398 (55.0%) |
| kills : deaths of fatalbot, seed 1 | 696 : 674 | 695 : 670 |
| G3 wall, stuck, seed 1 | 0.3%, 0.8% | 0.3%, 0.8% |

A difference of 1.5 points over 398 rounds is inside the spread of the seeds (`2026-11-13-feet.md`: under 5 points
needs 16 seeds or more for each side). The change is one of the look, as the step between bursts was: it does not
claim strength.
